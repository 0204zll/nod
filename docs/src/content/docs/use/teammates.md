Nod is your main assistant. Teammates are helpers you add for one job, such as bills and receipts or meal plans. Each has its own chat, name, avatar, model, memory and instructions. They use the same apps, Vault, rules and approvals as Nod.

You don't need teammates. If you never add one, Nod works exactly as before.

## Add a teammate

Select **+** next to **Teammates** in the sidebar. The new teammate opens its chat and sets itself up with you, one question at a time: its job, anything it should always or never do, its name and its model. Pick an answer or write your own. You can change any of it later.

## Talk to a teammate

Open a teammate in the sidebar and write in its chat. Its questions, approvals and answers stay in that chat.

In your chat with Nod, type **@** to bring in a teammate, for example "Did the electricity bill come in? If it did, have @Penny take care of it." Nod answers what it can and hands the rest over. Under its reply, **Handed to Penny** names the work, and **Open** opens Penny's chat. Nod also hands work to a teammate when the request clearly fits its job. When the teammate finishes, its result arrives in your chat with Nod as one sentence.

The sidebar shows each teammate's latest message. A dot marks new messages, and a pink dot marks a teammate that needs you.

## Change a teammate

Select the name at the top of a teammate's chat to open its settings. Select the name at the top of your chat with Nod for Nod's model.

| Setting      | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Avatar       | Select the picture to pick a shape and color, make a gradient from a few words about its colors, or upload an image.                                                                                                                                                 |
| Name         | Select the name to rename it. Each teammate needs its own name.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Model        | **Same as Nod**, or a model of its own. Its costs show in [Usage](/use/spending/).                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Memory       | **Shares Nod's memory**: reads and adds to Nod's notes. **Reads Nod's memory, keeps its own notes**: knows what Nod knows, and what it learns in its chat stays with it. **Own memory only**: never sees Nod's notes or your chat with Nod, and keeps its own notes. |
| Instructions | What it should always or never do. Instructions guide its work. They never approve an action.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Channels     | Send your [Telegram or WhatsApp](/use/channels/) messages to this teammate instead of Nod. It answers there with its name in front. A channel belongs to one teammate or to Nod.                                                                                     |

You can also ask a teammate, or Nod, to rename it or change its job, avatar or instructions.

**Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar lists Nod and every teammate under **Teammates**, with **New teammate**.

## Remove a teammate

Open its settings and select **Remove**, or right-click it in the sidebar. Removing stops its work. Its chat and its own notes go with it, and its channels go back to Nod.

## What stays with Nod

Teammates act through Nod. Every action a teammate takes goes through the same [trust check](/trust/approvals/), payments and sign-ins come to you for approval, and no teammate sees a saved password, code or card. A "yes" in one chat can't approve what another chat asked.
